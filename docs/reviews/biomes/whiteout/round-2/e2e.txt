   test passed in every run: nothing is saved on open.

     controlHints expectation, and it is reverted (src/core/settings.ts = b70a793 again). The test now requires
     reduceWeather: false after the save, exactly as controlHints: true (f51fd74 had made it optional; corrected).

   fix in 68aba34, as release.spec.ts already did).
